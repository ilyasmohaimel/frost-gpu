Frost GPU: unplug, keep going.

An experimental KDE session that moves rendering between Vega and the RX 6800M while supported apps stay open. Battery and USB-C prefer the iGPU; the barrel charger selects the RX.

Source and supported-app notes: https://github.com/ilyasmohaimel/frost-gpu
