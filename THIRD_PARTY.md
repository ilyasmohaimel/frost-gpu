  [ZrnSelectiveSuspend](https://github.com/z3r0n3br4instorm/ZrnSelectiveSuspend),
  under GPL-2.0-only. The patch includes the local render-only extension and

- ROG Control Center is an optional external build from
  [OpenGamingCollective/asusctl](https://github.com/OpenGamingCollective/asusctl).

This is an independent experiment, not an official KDE, AMD, NVIDIA, or ASUS
release. Their names identify the components and hardware used.

## Video

The storyboard and composition were created with the
[`/brag` skill](https://github.com/latent-spaces/brag), revision
`069432cac935ed1895859df201c31436c07fb74e`, and
[HyperFrames](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes).
The editable HTML uses GSAP. NPM dependencies are installed separately from
the pinned lockfile and retain their own licenses. HyperFrames and GSAP code
are not relicensed by the project license.

The video uses local system font fallbacks. No font files, private desktop
captures, or bundled third-party music are redistributed. Its short synthesized
audio track is generated by the included Python script. The animation depicts
the policy and measured outcomes; it is not footage or a performance benchmark.
