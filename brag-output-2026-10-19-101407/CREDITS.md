# Picture, sound and sources

## Laptop model

The final enclosure, display, keyboard, clickpad, hinges, vents and internal illustration are built as Blender geometry. The visible keyboard layout is traced from the exact ASUS G513QY product reference supplied by the user, cross-checked against the user's other photographs and the official gallery:

https://rog.asus.com/uk/laptops/rog-strix/2021-rog-strix-g15-advantage-edition-series/gallery/

ASUS master reference:
https://dlcdnwebimgs.asus.com/gain/FB052830-05EB-4A54-8727-E2D391A2755C/w1600/h1200

The model uses 87 individually traced low-profile key shapes plus a separate power button. This preserves the five hotkeys, short F row, shaped Enter, media navigation column, arrow cluster and original legends. A calibrated ASUS reference image supplies the key legends, RGB borders and deck pattern as model materials. It is not a photographic shot in the edit. The image remains ASUS copyright. Reference files and editable Blender scenes are kept in the owner's local asset cache; rendered camera footage and original build scripts are included in this delivery. Hidden dimensions are reconstructed from available views rather than manufacturing CAD.

The user's personal photographs are modelling references only. No personal photograph is used as footage or a texture, and no personal photographs are included in the deliverable assets.

## Hardware reference

ASUS G513/G533 service guide, revision 2023-12-06, page 25 for the G513QY motherboard layout:
https://dlcdnets.asus.com/pub/ASUS/GamingNB/G533QM/Service_guide_G513_G533_20231206--NR-R2R.pdf

The tight cutaway preserves the APU on the left, GPU on the right, six GDDR6 packages and rear power stages. The vapor chamber is retained with two local illustration openings over the APU and RX. The chassis opening reveals a narrow section of the inward-set right blower; the remaining housing stays under the deck. The right blower sits against the chamber, with only a narrow section exposed at the chassis edge. The board outline and raised passive locations are traced from the service photograph. The PCB finish is a procedural 3D material. The final close-up uses an RX-rendered still with a small camera push; the full 3D model and camera path remain in the production source. The packages, dies, mounting eyelets, inductors and passives are model geometry. This is an illustrated cutaway, not a repair schematic. The screen remains physically attached to the APU; the drawn curve indicates renderer ownership. Service photographs and the PDF remain outside the editable composition.

## Desktop capture

The moving content on the model display is a six-second recording of an isolated Brave window on this laptop, captured with FFmpeg from its XWayland window. It shows only the public repository and a scroll through its README. No private windows or account data are in the clip.

The paired images are real captures on this machine, with isolated Brave and VSCodium profiles and an owned WezTerm window. They show public project source and the public repository. The KWin renderer was switched using the existing controller's manual command, then restored to automatic policy. The fixed-iGPU OpenGL capture terminal stays open during the switch. These captures are not physical charger-switch footage, a switching-speed benchmark, or proof of RX power-off. The RX runtime status is active in both captures.
